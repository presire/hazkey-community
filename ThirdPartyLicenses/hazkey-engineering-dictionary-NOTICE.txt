This repository contains a curated list of Japanese engineering terms
(data/engineering_entries.tsv) and LOUDS dictionary shards compiled from it
(EngineeringDictionary/louds/). The readings and surfaces were collected from
the sources below. No share-alike licensed data (Wikipedia CC BY-SA,
OpenStreetMap ODbL, JMdict / COMPDIC CC BY-SA 4.0) was used.

  Used for: readings (ReadingForm) and surfaces (Headword / IndexForm)
